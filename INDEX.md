## 🚀 The 4 Tabs (What You Can Do)

### Tab 1: Deploy 🎯
**Purpose:** Generate tailored CVs for each job  
**Flow:** Upload CV → Paste JD → Generate → Download  
**Features:** 
- ✅ AI-powered tailoring (no fabrication)
- ✅ ATS scoring (target 80+)
- ✅ ⚠️ ATS warnings if below target
- ✅ PDF export
- ✅ Cover letter + email draft

**See:** README.md, QUICK_REFERENCE.md

---

### Tab 2: Pipeline 📊
**Purpose:** Track all your applications  
**Flow:** Log application → Edit status → Set follow-ups → Track  
**Features:**
- ✅ Company, role, date, status tracking
- ✅ ATS score display
- ✅ Follow-up date reminders (red alerts)
- ✅ Inline editing (auto-save)
- ✅ CV version tracking
- ✅ Stats dashboard

**See:** QUICK_REFERENCE.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md

---

### Tab 3: Runbook 📚
**Purpose:** Log interview questions, prep for rounds  
**Flow:** Log question → Tag by type/round → Track outcome → Review later  
**Features:**
- ✅ Question type (5 types)
- ✅ Interview round tracking
- ✅ Your answer/notes
- ✅ Outcome tracking (Strong/Okay/Weak/Follow-up)
- ✅ Color-coded badges
- ✅ Filter by company

**See:** IMPLEMENTATION_SUMMARY.md, QUICK_REFERENCE.md

---

### Tab 4: Base Profile 📄
**Purpose:** Manage your master CV  
**Flow:** Upload CV → Review extracted text → Save profile  
**Features:**
- ✅ PDF upload
- ✅ **DOCX upload** (NEW!)
- ✅ Manual paste
- ✅ Text extraction
- ✅ Version timestamp
- ✅ Auto-validation

**See:** SETUP.md, QUICK_REFERENCE.md

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** Yes! Everything stays on your computer. No cloud, no tracking.  
**See:** [README.md](README.md#-privacy--data)

### Q: What if I lose my data?
**A:** Export it anytime as JSON backup.  
**See:** [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md#-export-data-backup)

### Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?
**A:** Not automatically (browser-local only). Backup/restore manually via JSON.  
**See:** [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md#data-storage)

### Q: How much does it cost?
**A:** Free! Runs on your computer.  
**See:** [README.md](README.md)

### Q: Can I customize the LLM?
**A:** Yes, switch models in Ollama.  
**See:** [README.md](README.md#switch-to-faster-model)

### Q: What if Ollama is down?
**A:** You can still upload CVs and track applications, just can't generate new ones.  
**See:** [SETUP.md](SETUP.md#troubleshooting)
